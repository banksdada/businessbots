Using the AI‑OS Platform

A quick walkthrough for new users.

1️⃣ Create an account

Visit /register, enter your email and name. You’ll get a confirmation email to confirm your address.

2️⃣ Onboard your first business

After logging in, go to the onboarding wizard /onboarding. Provide a business name, address and one‑time payment if you choose the paid tier.

3️⃣ Connect calendars & messaging

No configuration is required for the demo. If you want to sync real bookings, connect Google or Outlook under Settings → Calendars. The AI will read your schedule and make suggestions.

4️⃣ Tell the AI what you need

In /problems/new record your issue. You can either type the description or tap the Record your problem button. The system will transcribe your speaking into text automatically.

5️⃣ Wait for the plan

After you hit “Find me a fix”, the AI will create a draft plan and email the owner. You’ll see the progress on /problems/show. Once the owner approves, the final report and next steps are sent to your inbox.

6️⃣ Go to the dashboard

Post‑review, you’ll have access to your customer requests, live stats, and lead management via /dashboard.
